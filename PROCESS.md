# Development Process
**Team:** - Cyberguys
**Project:** - SEMO Recreation Center Club & Meeting Calendar
**Course:** - CS445 

## Chosen Model: Scrum(Agile)

Our team selected **Scrum** as our Agile process model. We work in short
sprints, pull items from a product backlog, and review completed work at
the end of each sprint before planning the next. With a 3 person team and
requirements that will become clearer as we build, Scrum lets us deliver
and test the calendar in small increments rather than all at once.

- ** Sprint Length:** 2 weeks
- ** Sprint Goal:** - A basic working version that can store and display
  clubs and meeting information (dates, times, locations).

  ## Project Board
  The backlog and the spring progression are tracked on our Github project board
